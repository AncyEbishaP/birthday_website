# 🎂 Jesulin Raj S – Pastel Birthday Celebration Website

A personalized digital birthday gift website created with love, appreciation, and God's blessings for **Jesulin Raj S**.

## ✨ Highlights & Features

- **Thematic Pastel Aesthetic**: Designed with baby blue, ivory cream, soft lavender, light peach, subtle gold, and delicate pink accents.
- **Section 1: Hero Surprise**: Fullscreen hero with floating helium balloons, twinkling stars, and an animated birthday cake.
- **Section 2: The One & Only Jesulin**:
  - Centerpiece **Illustrated Tribute Portrait** (custom-generated matching his reference photo).
  - Photo toggle between the **Illustrated Art** and his **Original Photos**.
  - **Cinema VIP Badge & Clapperboard** celebrating his love for movies and storytelling.
  - 5 Pastel Appreciation Cards: *Cinema & Stories*, *Genuine Kindness*, *Sense of Humour*, *Faith in God*, and *Unique Personality*.
- **Section 3: A Blessing for Your New Year (Bible Verse)**:
  - Sacred golden ornament card featuring **Numbers 6:24–26 (KJV)** (The Priestly Blessing) and **Jeremiah 29:11 (ESV)**.
  - Heartfelt personal prayer for Jesulin.
  - Interactive *"Receive Blessing • Amen ✨"* button.
- **Section 4: A Few Words from Me (Personal Letter)**:
  - Textured parchment stationery with wax seal, handwriting script, and warm friendship note.
  - In-page *"Customize"* button and *"Copy Note"* feature.
- **Section 5: Birthday Celebration & Interactive Cake**:
  - Interactive candles that can be individually clicked or blown out (`Blow All Candles 💨`) with realistic smoke puffs and celebratory chimes.
  - Animated *"✨ Make a Wish!"* button that unleashes a vibrant pastel confetti explosion and opens a blessing modal.
- **Section 6: A Sweet Ending**:
  - Heartfelt closing blessings.
  - Nostalgic **Polaroid Memory Gallery** featuring his photos with washi tape styling.
- **Ambient Peaceful Audio**:
  - Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer playing a serene, acoustic music box birthday melody—requires zero external audio files and works offline on any device.

## 🚀 How to Open and View

### Option A: Double-Click (Zero Install)
Simply double-click `index.html` in Windows Explorer to open it directly in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or any modern web browser!

### Option B: Local Web Server
You can also launch a lightweight local server:
```powershell
npm start
```
Then open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.

---

## ✏️ How to Personalize or Edit

1. Click the **"Customize"** button in the top right corner of the website.
2. Edit his name, letter, or prayers directly in the modal.
3. Click **"Save Changes"** — your personalized version is saved right in your browser!
